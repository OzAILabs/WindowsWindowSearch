Sourdough basics for beginners

A good loaf takes four ingredients and a little patience.

  1. Feed your starter until it doubles in size.
  2. Mix flour, water, salt and starter, then rest for an hour.
  3. Fold the dough every 30 minutes for two hours.
  4. Shape it and leave it in the fridge overnight.
  5. Bake in a hot covered pot until deep brown.
