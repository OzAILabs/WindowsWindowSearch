WAYFARER

Lisbon in spring:
a 4-day itinerary

Day 1: AlfamaRide the old tram up the hill, then get lost in tiled lanes before dinner at a family tavern.
Day 2: BelémVisit the monastery and the riverside tower, with custard tarts straight from the oven.
Day 3: SintraTake the morning train to the painted palace and walk the forest gardens.
Day 4: Cais do SodréBrowse the food hall, take a ferry across the river, and catch the sunset from the far bank.
